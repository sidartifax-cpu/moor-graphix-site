Frequently Asked Questions

Publishing, answered.

Can Moor Graphix put my book on Amazon?
Moor Graphix can help prepare your book and guide you through publishing it using Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). Your KDP account and royalties remain under your control.
Can you publish both Kindle and paperback books?
Yes. Projects can be prepared for Kindle eBooks, paperback print-on-demand, or both depending on the manuscript and publishing goals.
Do I need to order hundreds of books?
No. Amazon KDP offers print-on-demand for eligible print books, allowing copies to be produced as they are ordered. Authors may also purchase author copies for events and direct distribution subject to Amazon's current terms and availability.
Can you design my book cover?
Yes. Moor Graphix can design a custom book cover as part of the project or prepare an existing approved design for the required publishing format.
Can you format the inside of my book?
Yes. Interior formatting can include chapter styling, typography, margins, page numbering, front matter, back matter, and other elements appropriate to the project.
Do you edit manuscripts?
Editing and proofreading are separate from standard publishing setup and should be quoted based on manuscript length and condition. Moor Graphix can review the project and determine what preparation is needed before formatting begins.
Can you help market the book after it is published?
Yes. Moor Graphix can create author branding, launch graphics, social media assets, promotional flyers, bookmarks, QR materials, landing pages, email creative, and other marketing materials as additional services.
How much does it cost to publish a book with Moor Graphix?
KDP setup assistance starts at $250, Kindle publishing packages start at $450, Kindle + paperback packages start at $750, and full author launch packages start at $1,500. Final pricing depends on manuscript length, condition, design requirements, illustrations, editing needs, and launch scope.
Does Moor Graphix guarantee book sales or bestseller status?
No. Moor Graphix can improve the professional presentation, discoverability foundation, and marketing assets surrounding a book, but no ethical publishing or marketing service can guarantee sales, rankings, reviews, or bestseller status.
