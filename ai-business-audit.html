Questions

Before you book.

How is the AI Business Audit different from the free AI Readiness Assessment?
The free assessment is a 10-minute self-scored quiz that flags where AI could help. The AI Business Audit is a paid, hands-on engagement — Moor Graphix maps your actual workflows, evaluates 8 areas of your business in depth, and delivers a full report with a prioritized opportunity matrix, quick wins, and a 90-day roadmap.
Which audit package is right for my business?
It depends on scope. The AI Opportunity Snapshot is a fast, focused look at your single biggest opportunity. The AI Systems Audit is the full audit across all 8 evaluation areas. The AI Operations Blueprint adds a complete 90-day implementation roadmap and rollout support. We'll help you pick the right fit on your free discovery call.
What do I actually receive at the end of the audit?
A full written report covering your Executive Summary, AI Readiness Score, Workflow Maps, AI Opportunity Matrix, Quick Wins, a 90-Day Roadmap, and an Implementation Proposal — plus a walkthrough call to review it with you.
Do you also implement the recommendations?
Yes. The Implementation Proposal included in every audit outlines exactly how Moor Graphix can execute the roadmap, but you're never obligated to move forward with us — the audit and report are yours either way.
How long does the audit take?
Timelines vary by package and the complexity of your operations. We'll confirm a specific timeline for your business during the free discovery call.
