Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to be technical to take this workshop?
Not at all. The workshop is designed for small business owners, creatives, and entrepreneurs — not engineers. You don't need to know how to code. You need to know your brand, your customers, and your goals. We build the technical layer together, step by step.
What AI tools are covered in the curriculum?
The core stack is Claude (AI reasoning and writing), Make.com (automation and agent workflows), and Notion (knowledge base and Brand OS). We also cover supplemental tools as the curriculum evolves. Everything is chosen for small business usability — nothing enterprise-priced or overcomplicated.
Is this workshop only for designers and creatives?
No. The Cultural AI Alchemy framework applies to any small business with a brand identity to protect. Coaches, consultants, service providers, educators, nonprofits, and product businesses have all gone through this curriculum. If you have a story, a community, and a voice — this is for you.
How much time should I invest each week?
Plan for 4–6 hours per week: the live session (Foundation: self-paced, 1.5–2 hrs), homework build time (2–3 hrs), and optional community engagement. The live cohort sessions run 90 minutes each. Recordings are always available if you need to catch up.
What will I have built by the end of Week 4?
A working AI system for your brand: your Brand OS document, a voice-locked prompt library, at least one automated content pipeline, and a deployed intake agent. VIP Intensive clients also receive a custom agent built specifically for their brand by Sid. You leave with infrastructure — not just ideas.
What's the difference between the self-paced Foundation and the live Workshop?
Foundation gives you the full 4-week curriculum at your own pace — ideal if you prefer to work independently and have a flexible schedule. The Workshop adds 4 live weekly sessions with Sid, hot seat coaching where you build in real time, a private cohort community, and 30 days of post-workshop Q&A access. If you want accountability and direct feedback, the Workshop is worth the upgrade.
