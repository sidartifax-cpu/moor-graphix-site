    Questions

    Before you start.

    Is the AI Readiness Assessment really free?
    Yes. The AI Readiness Assessment is completely free with no obligation. It's designed to identify opportunities, not replace a full paid consulting engagement — that's what our AI Business Audit is for.
    How long does the assessment take?
    Most business owners finish in under 10 minutes. It's 15 short questions across five sections: your business, daily operations, customers, marketing, and technology.
    Do I need any technical knowledge to take it?
    No. The questions are about how your business currently runs day to day — not about AI or software. No technical background is required.
    What happens after I submit my answers?
    You'll immediately see your preliminary AI Readiness Score and sub-scores on screen, along with your biggest identified opportunity. You'll also get the option to book a free 20-minute AI Strategy Call to review the results in detail.
    What's the difference between the free assessment and the paid AI Business Audit?
    The free assessment is a quick health check that flags where AI and automation could help. The paid AI Business Audit goes much deeper — a full workflow analysis, AI opportunity mapping, a customized implementation roadmap, technology recommendations, estimated ROI, and a 30/60/90-day action plan.
    Will I be pressured to buy something?
    No. The assessment and strategy call are both free with no obligation. If a paid engagement makes sense for your business, we'll say so — but there's no pressure either way.
