The Fastest Path: Start With Audio

If the budget is tight, do not chase a fancy camera first. A strong voice is the drum. Clean sound makes people stay. Start with a USB microphone, headphones, and free recording software.

Bedroom Sound Wisdom

  • Record near soft things: curtains, bed, clothing, rugs, pillows, and blankets.
  • Keep the microphone 4–8 inches from your mouth.
  • Turn off fans, TVs, air conditioners, and loud devices.
  • Do a 30-second test before every episode.
  • Use OBS Studio for video recording and Audacity or GarageBand for audio-only episodes.
