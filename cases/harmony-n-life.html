01 — The Label

An independent label
that believed in
the music.

Harmony N Life Records built their name on the power of live performance — hosting regular showcases, jam sessions, and ticketed events that brought audiences together around authentic jazz and R&B. From their earliest shows in 2006 through a decade of events, they ran a consistent live music program that demanded consistent, high-quality creative.

Moor Graphix became the label's creative partner from the start — designing monthly event flyers, promotional materials, and building the visual identity that accompanied every show. As the label grew its artist roster, that partnership extended into album design for their flagship vocalist.

The relationship lasted 8 years. Every flyer, every album, every event — the same standard of creative that honored what the music deserved.

01

Monthly event flyers — 2006 through 2009

Designed every monthly event flyer for Harmony N Life's regular live music showcase series — a consistent visual brand that grew the label's audience season over season.

02

Album artwork for Rhonda Thomas

Designed the full discography of album covers for Rhonda Thomas, the label's primary recording artist — from concept and layout through press-ready production.

03

Specialty event promotion

Produced promotional creative for HNL's landmark events — the Grammy's Night viewing party, Rhonda's DC and Blue Note performances, and the 2014 Prince tribute concert.

04

Print production & press-ready delivery

Every deliverable produced to professional print standards — PDF press files, color-accurate proofing, and multiple size variants for print, digital, and social distribution.

Harmony N Life ran their label on belief — belief in their artists, in their community, and in the power of live music. Moor Graphix brought that same belief to every deliverable: album covers that stood on any shelf, event flyers that sold tickets, and a visual presence that said this label took its work seriously.

The Approach

Design that
elevates the artist.

Working with an independent label means the creative has to work harder. There's no major-label marketing machine behind the rollout — the art is often the entire first impression.

Moor Graphix brought the same level of craft to every Harmony N Life project that a major label would expect from their top agencies — because the music was that good, and the audience deserved to see that from the cover before they ever hit play.
