The Vision

This is more than a film.

In November 2024, you chose yourself. You left the country, the roles, the noise — and you went to find out who you were without any of it. That decision deserves to be told in a way that moves people. A way that opens doors, fills rooms, and anchors everything you build from here.

The Gone Girl Brand Story Film is your visual origin story. The documentary that every future client, speaking booker, publisher, and retreat attendee watches before they decide you are exactly who they need.

Story Architecture

Five acts. One transformation.

I Act One
The Decision

Why she left. The weight of what she was walking away from. The moment everything changed.

II Act Two
The Arrival

Starting over in Panama. The disorientation of freedom. Learning a new country and a new self.

III Act Three
The Adventure

Travel. Excursions. Friendships. The joy of discovery when you have no obligation but to live.

IV Act Four
The Reality

Loneliness. Healing. Growth. The parts that don't make it to social media — the real work.

V Act Five
The Transformation

What changed. Who she became. What comes next. The woman standing on the other side of that decision.
