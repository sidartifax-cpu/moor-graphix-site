Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 25, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Moor Graphix ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you visit our website at moorgraphix.com, contact us, or use our services — including our SMS messaging program. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency and care.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us, request a quote, book a consultation, or purchase a service, you may provide:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (including mobile number)
  • Business name and project details
  • Any other information you choose to share

Information Collected Through SMS Opt-In

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from Moor Graphix, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The method and timestamp of your opt-in consent
  • Message delivery and interaction data
  • Opt-out requests and the date received

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and deliver proposals
  • Provide the services you've engaged us for
  • Send project updates and scheduling confirmations
  • Schedule and conduct consultations
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations
SMS Program

SMS-Specific Use

Your mobile number and SMS consent data are used exclusively to:

  • Send you messages you have consented to receive — including appointment reminders, project updates, and promotional offers from Moor Graphix
  • Maintain TCPA consent records as required by law
  • Process opt-out requests promptly
No selling or sharing of SMS data

Moor Graphix does not sell, share, rent, or transfer your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data to third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

3. SMS Messaging Program

TCPA Compliance

Consent

We only send SMS messages to individuals who have provided express written consent via an opt-in form on our website or during a client intake process. Consent is never assumed, inferred, or purchased.

Message Types

Messages may include appointment reminders, project milestone notifications, service announcements, and promotional offers from Moor Graphix.

Message Frequency

We send up to 4 messages per month. Frequency may vary based on project status and your engagement with our services.

Costs

Moor Graphix does not charge for SMS messages. However, message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier and plan. Contact your carrier for details.

How to Opt Out

You may stop receiving SMS messages from Moor Graphix at any time by replying STOP to any message. After sending STOP, you will receive one final confirmation text acknowledging your opt-out. No further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.

You may also opt out by emailing sidart@moorgraphix.com with your mobile number and a removal request, or by calling (470) 499-4407.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance. You may also contact us at sidart@moorgraphix.com or (470) 499-4407.

Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is dependent on carrier network availability.

Data Retention & Security

We retain SMS opt-in consent records for a minimum of 4 years as required for TCPA compliance. These records are stored securely and are not used for any purpose beyond compliance documentation and service delivery.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers — including web hosting, email delivery, appointment scheduling, and SMS delivery platforms — who assist us in operating our business. These providers are bound by agreements that require them to protect your data.

We may disclose your information where required by law, court order, or government regulation.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and understand visitor behavior. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP to any message

To exercise these rights, contact us at sidart@moorgraphix.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at sidart@moorgraphix.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
