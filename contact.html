Questions

Before you reach out.

How do I get started on a project?
The easiest way is to book a free 30-minute consultation using the booking link above. We'll talk about your project, your goals, your timeline, and your budget. From there, Sid will put together a custom proposal — no templates, no generic pricing sheets. Every engagement is scoped specifically for you.
Do you work with clients outside of Georgia?
Absolutely. Moor Graphix is a remote-first studio. We've delivered projects for clients in New York, Los Angeles, Washington D.C., and cities across the country. All discovery, strategy, and design work happens digitally — which means wherever you are, we can serve you at the same level as any local client.
What's included in the Brand Blueprint?
Every engagement includes a Brand Blueprint — a complete roadmap for your visual identity, brand voice, and digital presence. It covers how to use your brand consistently across every touchpoint: social media, web, print, email, apparel, and AI. It's the operating manual for your brand, not just a style guide. This is how your investment compounds over time.
How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary by scope. A logo + brand identity system typically runs 2–4 weeks. A full website project is usually 4–8 weeks depending on complexity and content readiness. AI agent builds and integrations are scoped individually. Rush timelines are available for select projects — ask about availability during your consultation.
What if I don't know exactly what I need?
That's exactly what the free consultation is for. Come with a direction — even a vague one — and Sid will help you clarify what makes the most sense given your goals, audience, and resources. You don't need a brief. You just need to show up and talk about your vision. We'll figure out the rest together.
